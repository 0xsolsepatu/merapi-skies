Merapi Skies

Island Bush Pilot

Fly a bush plane around Pulau Merapi — a volcanic island of grass strips, coral coasts and crater villages. Mind your throttle, your fuel and your landings: the island forgives a gentle pilot and none other.

Pick a mission (keys 1–4), then press Enter or Start. No network, no account — just fly.
